You put in the hours. This time is yours. One clock at 5:00, quitting time, with the hands standing outside the O.

The logos and where they go

One-line logo

Hat fronts, the website header, packaging, anything wide.

Embroidered: letters 7 mm tall (62.6 mm wide). Screens: 100 px wide.

Stacked logo

Every square or circle: profile pictures, app icon, Shopify square logo, round stickers.

Screens: 40 px. Below that, the tab icon.

Clock

The back or side of a hat, patches, die-cut stickers. Never alone in a square.

Embroidered: 19 mm tall (side master below that). Screens: 48 px tall.

Tab icon

The browser tab only. Drawn pixel by pixel at 16, 32 and 48 px.

Use the size it was drawn for; never scale it.

Type

OFF SHFT

Unbounded Bold, capitals, letter-spacing 0.12 em: the logo, headings and every phrase on a hat. Embroidered letters at least 6.6 mm tall.

Archivo for everything else you read on the website.

Both are free Google Fonts.

Space around it

Leave at least the height of the letters clear all round, measured from the tips of the hands.

Don't

  • Put the hands inside the ring, crop them or shorten them.
  • Put the clock alone in a square. Squares get the stacked logo.
  • Stretch, redraw, outline, add shadows or set the name in another font.
  • Use colour pairs other than the ones in the fabric table.
  • Scale the tab icon, or use the embroidery files as screen art (or the reverse).
Thread colours are targets: match each on a physical thread card (Isacord 40 or Madeira Polyneon 40) and approve from a sew-out. Files and which one to use: the brand folder's README. Embroidery masters and stitch notes: designs/mark/final/the-o.
