You put in the hours. This time is yours. One clock at 5:00, quitting time, with the hands standing outside the O.

The logos and where they go

One-line logo

Hat fronts, the website header, packaging, anything wide.

Embroidered: 62.6 mm wide (letters 10.7 mm, the floor is 10 mm), from the drawing made for that size. Screens: 80 px wide.

Stacked logo

Every square or circle: profile pictures, app icon, Shopify square logo, round patches and stickers.

Drawn for 40 px and up. Smaller, the app shows the name beside it.

Clock

Hat backs and sides (the side master there), patches and stickers cut to its outline. Not the main profile picture.

Embroidered: 19 mm tall in two colours, 20.5 mm in one, each from the drawing made for that size. Screens: 48 px tall.

Tab icon

The browser tab only. Redrawn on the pixel grid at 16, 32 and 48 px (shown at 48).

Use the size it was drawn for; never scale it.

Type

PUNCHED OUT.

Unbounded Bold, capitals, letter-spacing 0.12 em: headings and the phrases on hats; the logo's letters are drawn, not typed. Embroidered letters at least 6.6 mm tall, with the S trims: phrases are set with the build, never typed.

Archivo for website text and labels.

Both are free Google Fonts.

Space around it

Around the one-line logo, and the stacked logo on a square: at least the height of the letters, measured from the tips of the hands. In circles the stacked logo fills 90% of the radius on purpose.

Don't

  • Put the hands inside the ring, crop them or shorten them. Only the tiny side and tab drawings shorten them.
  • Make the clock alone your profile picture or app icon. Squares get the stacked logo.
  • Stretch, redraw, outline, add shadows or set the name in another font.
  • Use colour pairs other than the ones in the fabric table.
  • Scale an embroidery file or the tab icon: each size has its own drawing.
Thread colours are targets. On Printful's standard threads they come out as the nearest thread above; Printful's unlimited-colour embroidery (about US$3.95 more a hat) matches the hex.
