  9. Leave the stock alone: the 9,999 at the Printful location is how Printful marks a linked variant.

File rules

  • Upload the PNG the card gives you. Embroidery: the 600 dpi file in Printful's thread colours. Print: the 300 dpi file in brand colours. Both are true size with a transparent background.
  • Set the width in inches from the card. Never resize an embroidery file to another size: each size is its own drawing. Print files can scale.
  • Printful digitizes the PNG itself; no stitch file needed. Up to 6 threads per placement, flat colours only.
  • Printful's minimums: text 6.35 mm tall, lines 1.27 mm. Its disclaimer adds that shapes under 3 mm apart may be joined by connecting stitches, and every design here has letter gaps of 1.5 to 2.4 mm and channels of 1.0 to 1.5 mm. The logo's and clock's finest parts are also under the 1.27 mm line minimum: check them all on a sew-out.
  • 3D puff: hat fronts only (catalogue copies also list the Cuffed Beanie; the RIO is disputed). Lines 5 to 12.5 mm, gaps at least 1.27 mm, and no flat stitching in the same placement.
  • Areas: 6245CM front 5.5 x 2 in; 6606 front 5.9 x 2 in; 31-069 front 6.3 x 2.55 in; hat side or back 2 x 1 in; beanie cuff 5 x 1.75 in; left or centre chest 4 x 4 in; sleeve 2 x 3 in; large front, centre or back 10 x 6 in. Each product's File guidelines tab has the final word.
