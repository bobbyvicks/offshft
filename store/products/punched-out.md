## Design status

- Current draft: designs/09-punched-out/. One line, 78.5 × 4.8 mm, set in Archivo Expanded Bold before the brand font was chosen. Slate fabric with Bone thread; Bone fabric with Rust thread.
- Measured in Unbounded Bold at 6.6 mm cap height and 0.12 em letter-spacing, with the repo's own kit (tools/kit/kit.cjs): one line is 94.0 mm wide. On two lines, PUNCHED is 60.4 mm and OUT. is 28.6 mm.
- 6.6 mm is the brand's floor for Unbounded. If the chosen partner asks for taller text, every width here grows in step. Printful's minimum is 0.25 in (6.35 mm), so 6.6 mm passes. Printify's embroidery guide asks for 0.3 in (7.6 mm) capitals, though its help centre says 0.25 in. At 7.6 mm, measured the same way, one line is 108.2 mm, wider than Printify's 4 in (102 mm) cap area; on two lines PUNCHED is 69.6 mm and OUT. is 32.9 mm.
- Front area: Printful's is bigger than the 4 in in BRAND.md: about 5.9 × 2 in on the trucker and 5.5 × 2 in on the dad hat. So one line fits at Printful. The open question is whether 94 mm still looks small.
- Seams: on 6-panel caps the centre seam would run through the letters, the same reason the logo shifts 5.2 mm right. [CONFIRM: final layout checked against the centre seam of the chosen blank, or a 5-panel or seamless-front blank chosen]
- Trademark: the design's notes record that it replaced CLOCKED OUT, a filed US trademark for hats. They don't record a check on PUNCHED OUT itself. [CONFIRM: CIPO and USPTO search for PUNCHED OUT on hats and clothing, plus the Etsy, Amazon and Google Images search in DESIGNS.md]
- DESIGNS.md status is "owner likes" (keep for drop 1). It goes live only after the layout is final, the checks pass, a sample passes and the owner publishes it.
