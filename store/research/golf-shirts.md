# Golf shirts: Printful polos and the back-of-neck spot

Researched 29 Oct 2026 for the owner's ask: golf tees with Punched Out. and the clock on the back near the neck. Search results only (printful.com is blocked from these sessions). The shortlist is on the picker page.

## 1. In short

No Printful polo has an embroidery spot just below the collar in any source found. The 1717 and 6030 tees don't have one either. The only thing Printful puts right under a collar is a printed neck label, and none of the sources shows it on a polo. The closest stitched option is the "Large back" embroidery area on the SOL'S 11362 cotton polo, with the clock at the top centre. Three things about it are still unknown: how close to the collar it starts, what it adds to the price, and whether Printful makes this polo in North America (third-party data says Latvia). So the shortlist is three polos that look and feel different:
- **SOL'S 11362:** thick cotton, and the only one that might carry the clock on the back.
- **Port Authority K500:** the classic smooth golf shirt.
- **adidas A430:** light performance polyester.

On the K500 and the A430 the clock goes on the left sleeve instead. All three carry the stacked Punched Out. (63.59 × 18.00 mm) on the left chest. Nothing could be re-checked in this pass: the run's search budget was already used up and printful.com is blocked here. So the SOL'S checks in section 4 have to happen before anyone draws a back file.

## 2. The shortlist

**Why these three:**
- One is cotton, one is poly-cotton and one is performance polyester, so the cards look different at a glance.
- The SOL'S is the only polo with any embroidered back.
- If the SOL'S fails its checks, the K500 is the likely North American golf shirt.
- I picked the adidas A430 over the Under Armour 1370399, although the skeptic ranked the UA higher. adidas's logo is on the sleeve, while UA's is on the right chest facing Punched Out. The A430 comes in S–4XL instead of S–2XL, and it costs about US$10 less.

**Left off:**
- Gildan 64800: a second, plainer cotton pique. It's the stand-in if the SOL'S turns out to be made only in Europe.
- 64800L and UA 1370431: women's cuts.
- UA 1370399: see above.
- adidas A591: it costs more, and Printful warns that its light fabric can crumple around embroidery.

## 3. Placement plan

**Front (all three polos)**
- **Artwork:** Punched Out., stacked and centred, 63.59 × 18.00 mm (cap height 7 mm), at true size and never scaled.
  - Light polos: /home/user/offshft/designs/09-punched-out/v3/stacked-centred-light.svg (Ink letters, Rust full stop).
  - Dark polos: /home/user/offshft/designs/09-punched-out/v3/stacked-centred-dark.svg (Bone letters, Sun full stop).
- **Where:** Printful's "Left chest" spot (4 × 4 in, 101.6 mm), centred across it with 19.0 mm clear each side, the same as on the anorak.
  - The preview drawing /home/user/offshft/store/previews/items/polo.cjs puts its centre 95 mm left of the centre front and 190 mm below the shoulder line, about level with the third button.
  - Printful's own left-chest position decides the real spot.
- **Not the one-line version:** at 97.47 mm wide it leaves only 2.06 mm each side, and it can't shrink below the 6.6 mm minimum cap height.

**The clock**
- **Light fabric:** /home/user/offshft/designs/mark/final/the-o/symbol-light-25mm.svg (11.80 × 25.00 mm).
- **Dark fabric:** /home/user/offshft/designs/mark/final/the-o/symbol-dark.svg (also 25 mm).
- **Orientation:** upright, 12 o'clock at the top.
- **Smallest allowed:** 19 mm (/home/user/offshft/designs/mark/final/the-o/symbol-light-19mm.svg, 8.93 × 19.00 mm). Nothing smaller.
- **Digitizing:** it needs its own file at this size, US$6.50 once; Printful waives the fee on orders of 25 or more embroidered items.

**SOL'S 11362: the clock on the back**
- **Spot:** centred, at the very top of "Large back".
- **Target:** clock centre 45 mm below the back neck seam. That puts its top about 32 mm below the seam, and about 20 mm below the edge of the folded collar. This matches /home/user/offshft/store/previews/items/polo-back.cjs, where the collar hangs 13 mm below the seam.
- **Check in the editor:** record the real gap Printful allows.
- **If the area starts too low:** if the clock's top would land more than about 50 mm (2 in) below the seam, it reads as upper back rather than neck. The 50 mm line is my judgement, not a Printful rule. Then the owner decides: keep it there, or move it to the left sleeve top.

**K500 and A430: the clock on the sleeve**
- **K500:** centred on "Left sleeve top" (2 × 3 in, 50.8 × 76.2 mm). Either sleeve is offered; the left one matches the 6030 tee's sleeve clock.
- **A430:** "Left sleeve top" only, because the right sleeve carries adidas's logo. Keep the clock at 25 mm on this light fabric.

**Exceptions**
- **SOL'S Large back fails:** if it isn't offered for Canada and the US, or starts too low, move the clock to its left sleeve top. Printful lists both sleeve tops on this polo.
- **SOL'S made only in Europe:** swap it for the Gildan 64800 (100% cotton), with the clock on the sleeve.
- **A printed neck label:** that is Printful's "Outside label" (DTG, up to 3 × 3 in, US$2.49). None of the sources shows it on a polo, and it would break the "everything embroidered" rule.

**For the drawing agents**
- polo.cjs only has "chest" and "backNeck" spots. The K500 and A430 need a "leftSleeve" spot like the one in /home/user/offshft/store/previews/items/_tee.cjs, and their back view shows no mark.
- Hold any SOL'S back upload file until the first check in section 4 is done.
