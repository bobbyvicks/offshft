# OFF SHFT: trademark check on the four new designs

## In short

No filing for any of the four phrases turned up in the US or Canada in classes 25, 26 or 35. No one was found selling clothing under any of them.

- **Time Well Earned.** (t-shirt): **Looks clear.** No filing or seller was found, but the closest live registration, TIME WELL SPENT (US Reg. 3252699), has unknown goods, so open that record first.
- **The Rest Is Ours.** (hoodie): **Looks clear.** No filing or seller was found, and the nearest mark is a UK podcast company's THE REST IS, which is registered only in the EU and not shown for clothing.
- **Ours to Spend.** (trucker hat): **Looks clear.** No filing, seller or web page with the phrase turned up; many clothing owners use OURS alone, but none of them uses TO SPEND.
- **5:00** (crewneck): **Small risk.** Nothing is filed for "5:00" itself, but Margaritaville has sued sellers of "It's Five O'Clock Somewhere" apparel, and there are two nearby clothing marks: MISS 5 O'CLOCK (US, may have lapsed) and "5 a 7" (live in Canada).

Good to know: a slogan printed large on clothing usually reads as decoration, not a brand. That makes these phrases harder for anyone to register for clothing, OFF SHFT included, unless the phrase is also used as a brand on labels or tags. It does not stop a mark owner from objecting (see 5:00).

What the confidence column means:
- **Snippet:** the details were read only in search-result text. No record was opened.
- **Unverified:** the details conflict, or the record could not be found again.

**In the market:** No clothing brand called 5:00 was found. Margaritaville sells "It's Five O'Clock Somewhere" shirts. It also opposed IT'S 1700 HOURS SOMEWHERE, a reworded version filed for beer. Margaritaville sued online sellers of apparel with the slogan in Illinois. In April 2025 the court refused to dismiss the case ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/case-trademarks-procedure-n-d-ill-2)). Jaya sells clothing as Cinq à Sept, a US label launched in 2016.

**To stay clear:**
- Keep the product title, tags, description and design free of "somewhere", the song, and margarita, parrot or beach imagery.
- Call the product "5:00", not "5 O'Clock".
- Before marketing the crewneck in Quebec, get a lawyer's view on "5 a 7".

## Check these yourself

**USPTO Trademark Search**, https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/. Type each search, then filter to live marks in classes 25, 26 and 35:
- `time well earned`, `well earned`, `time well`
- `rest is ours`, `rest is yours`, `the rest is`, plus the owner name `Goalhanger`
- `ours to spend`, `yours to spend`
- `5:00`, `500`, `five o'clock`, `5 o'clock`, plus the owner name `Margaritaville Enterprises` (class 25 only)

**USPTO TSDR**, https://tsdr.uspto.gov/. Enter each serial number and press Status. Note the owner, the goods and whether it is live or dead:
- Time Well Earned: 76655835 (do this one first), 99224314, 88650604
- The Rest Is Ours: 86687604, 97872194, 98726473, 97365512 (REST IS RESISTANCE, goods unknown)
- Ours to Spend: 88704015, 90367974, 88841096, 98440843
- 5:00: 86951161, 86982356, 86695863, 86840252, 86182656

**CIPO Canadian Trademarks Database**, https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/srch. Type each:
- `time well earned`, `well earned`, `time well`
- `the rest is ours`, `rest is ours`, `the rest is`, `le reste est à nous`
- `ours to spend`, `yours to spend`
- `5:00`, `5 à 7`, `cinq à sept`, `five o'clock`, `it's five o'clock somewhere`
- Then open records 1784910 and 2232131 (Jaya) to read their goods and status.

Also worth doing:
- Search Etsy and Amazon quickly for each phrase.
- Get Margaritaville's Illinois complaint from PACER or CourtListener. It should list the registrations Margaritaville relies on.

- **Recent and design-only marks may be missing.** Filings from 2025–2026 and design-only marks are poorly indexed.
- **Canadian coverage is thin.** It covers only the CIPO pages a search engine had indexed. French versions of the phrases were not searched.
