added to the store: adding a listing, changing a price, a policy or the theme is the owner's call.

How it was done (workflow run wf_9c2e0411-502): two researchers (tops and layers; headwear and accessories), each checked
by a skeptic who tried to refute every claim; two line writers, a strict editor, a trademark knock-out screen and a
double-check of it. Search results and public copies of Printful's catalogue data only: printful.com, USPTO and CIPO
can't be read from these sessions. Prices use the store's rate, US$1 = C$1.42, and the margin rules in
store/products/drop1b-spec.json (Shopify Basic card fees; a C$10 profit floor on a US card, C$15 for hoodies; free
shipping from C$75). The full findings are in the appendix below.
