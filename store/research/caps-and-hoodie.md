## Retro Trucker Hat | Yupoong 6606 (YP Classics 6606 Retro Trucker Cap)

**product**: Retro Trucker Hat | Yupoong 6606 (YP Classics 6606 Retro Trucker Cap), Printful catalog product 252 [S2]

**printful url**: https://www.printful.com/custom/hats/trucker-hats/retro-trucker-cap-yupoong-6606 (same product: https://www.printful.com/custom/embroidered/trucker-hats/retro-trucker-cap-yupoong-6606 ; Canada: https://www.printful.com/ca/custom/embroidered/trucker-hats/retro-trucker-cap-yupoong-6606). www.printful.com does not resolve from this session; it has to be allowed in the session's network settings before the live page can be read.

**printful price**: US$13.56 on Printful's US listing. This figure was read through search results from the page's structured data, not from the page itself [S2]; Printful's bestsellers page shows the same figure [S3]. It appears to include the front embroidery. Printful says an embroidered product's base price includes one placement [S10]. One search read of this listing reported "one embroidery placement included" [S2]. Not included: one-time digitizing (up to US$6.50 per design, free on 25+ embroidered hats in one order), unlimited-colour embroidery (+US$3.95 a hat) [S11], and shipping (from US$4.69 in the US) [S2][S12]. Other figures: an older Printful blog lists US$16.88 for standard embroidery and US$21.42 for unlimited colour [S9]. The bestsellers snapshot also shows a second figure beside US$13.56 that the results don't explain [S3]. On Printful's Canadian site, one snapshot shows CA$19.25 [S5] and another shows CA$17.95 [S4].

**colours at printful**: NOTE: Printful's colour picker could not be read (site blocked). Printful's own pages give only a count, 13 colours, in older blog posts [S8][S9]. The 20 names below come from two places: a store that syncs from Printful and uses Printful's own description text (19 names) [S17], and a third-party chart of Printful's 6606 colours, which adds White (20) [S20][S21].; Black; White; Navy; Charcoal; Dark Heather Grey; Silver; Khaki; Caramel; Evergreen; Cranberry; Red; Pink; Black/White; Navy/White; Charcoal/Black; Heather/Black; Heather Grey/White; Brown/Khaki; Rustic Orange/Khaki; Moss/Khaki

**fabric**: Printful's current description: 26% cotton, 74% polyester, with a mesh back. This text appears on Printful-hosted stores and a Printful-synced store [S17][S18][S19], which also say the blank is "sourced from Vietnam or Bangladesh" [S17]. Older Printful text says 60% cotton, 40% polyester [S9]. The maker (Flexfit) lists 65% polyester / 35% cotton, with a note that the content changed to 74% polyester / 26% cotton [S22]. The 2-tone 6606T is listed at 74/26 [S23]. A distributor calls this a rolling change, so stock may be mixed [S24].

**panels**: 6 panels. The front is two panels sewn together down a centre seam, backed with hard buckram; the sides and back are mesh [S9][S22][S23][S25]. The 5-panel version (Yupoong 6506) has one front panel and no seam [S25].

**profile and structure**: Structured. The maker calls it mid profile, with a 3 1/2 in (8.9 cm) crown and a hard buckram front [S22][S23]. Printful describes it as a structured six-panel hat with a 3.5 in (8.9 cm) crown and hard buckram front panels [S9][S17]. Printful's embroidery-area help article, however, files it under "low-profile hats" [S6].

**closure**: Adjustable plastic snap closure (snapback) in a matching colour. Printful calls it a "plastic snap closure" or "adjustable closure" [S9][S18]; Flexfit says "snap, matching snapback" [S23].

**visor**: Permacurv pre-curved visor with a matching undervisor [S17][S18]. The maker adds 8 rows of stitching on the bill [S23].

**size range**: One size. Printful gives a head circumference of 21 5/8 to 23 5/8 in (54.9 to 60 cm) [S9][S18]. Flexfit says one size fits most, hat sizes 6 5/8 to 7 5/8 [S23].

**front embroidery area**: 5.9 x 2 in (15 x 5.08 cm). Printful's help article lists the Retro Trucker Hat | Yupoong 6606 among hats whose front area grew from 4 x 1.75 in (10.16 x 4.45 cm) to 5.9 x 2 in, and new designs default to the larger area [S6]. Printful's trucker FAQ gives the same general figure of about 5.9 x 2 in [S7]. Printful digitizes every design itself and points to the embroidery templates in each product's File guidelines tab [S6][S12].

**logo fit**: It fits. At 62.6 x 18.0 mm, the logo uses about 42% of the 150 mm width and 35% of the 50.8 mm height. Shifted 5.2 mm right, it runs from 26.1 mm left of centre to 36.5 mm right of centre, well inside the area's 75 mm either side. It would also fit the old 4 x 1.75 in area. The 19.0 mm-tall one-colour version fits as well. The front does have a centre seam: it is a 6-panel cap whose two front panels are sewn together down the middle [S25][S13]. So the notes.md rule applies: shift the lockup 5.2 mm right so the seam falls in the space between OFF and SHFT. Distributors warn the centre seam can disturb fine detail placed on it [S26]. Some Printful reviewers say their logo came out crooked or cramped where the seams meet [S2]. Printful's guide describes designs on 6-panel caps as centred across the two front panels [S13], so whether Printful keeps the 5.2 mm offset is unconfirmed (see below).

**other placements**: None found. A search read of Printful's listing shows only "Embroidery, Front" as customization options [S2], and the sides and back of this hat are mesh [S9]. Printful says side and back placements depend on the hat type and are listed in each product's File guidelines tab [S12]. On hats that offer them, Printful charges US$2.95 per extra placement [S10]. No price applies here unless the tab shows a side or back option.

**made in canada facility**: Nothing found says this hat is made in Canada. Printful sells it on its Canadian site with a CAD price, shown in stock [S4][S5]. Printful's Mississauga, Ontario facility opened making embroidered hats and apparel [S14][S15]. But Printful picks the facility itself, and says what Canada can produce varies by product [S14][S16]. To check, set the selling region to Canada and open the Availability tab for this hat.

**brand matches**: Mapped by name, plus the maker's Pantone (TCX) codes so each can be checked against a swatch. No swatch has been checked. In a Pantone TCX code, the first pair of digits is lightness on an 11-19 scale; higher is darker [S29]. In two-tone names, the first colour is the front panels and visor and the second is the mesh [S27].

- Bone #E4DDCB: no match. White is the only light colour, and it is brighter and colder.
- Oat #C7B48E: Khaki (solid; Flexfit 17-1312 [S22]) is the closest name. Its 17 lightness band suggests it may look darker than Oat.
- Moss #5A5F40: Moss/Khaki is the only moss, and it is two-tone, not solid: a moss front and visor (Flexfit 19-0516, which suggests darker than brand Moss [S23]) with khaki mesh.
- Spruce #2E3D35: Evergreen (Flexfit 19-5513 [S22], which a Pantone stockist lists as "Dark Green" [S28]) is likely the closest of all.
- Slate #27303D: Navy (bluer) or Charcoal (greyer). Fair at best.
- Rust #8E4426: Rustic Orange/Khaki is the closest name: an orange front (Flexfit 18-1447 [S23]) with khaki mesh. Caramel (17-1045) is lighter and more golden, and Cranberry (19-1726) is a deep red [S22].
