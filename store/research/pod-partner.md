# Print-on-demand partner: Printful or Printify

Research for OFF SHFT, 28 October 2026. This is a recommendation; the owner decides.

How this was checked: the partners' own sites could not be opened from this research session (network block). Every partner figure below comes from search results that quote the pages listed under Sources. Prices are in US dollars as the partners list them, unless marked C$. Some pages may be out of date. Anything marked [CONFIRM: ...] needs checking in the partner's catalog or dashboard before anyone relies on it.

## Recommendation

**Start with Printful.**

Why, in plain words:

- **It makes hats on both sides of the border.** Printful runs its own shops in the US and in Ontario, and sends each order to the closest shop that has the item [16][17]. A Canadian buyer can get a parcel from Ontario with no customs, and a US buyer gets one from the US. On Printify, each product is made in the shop you pick. Its automatic split by country lists basic tees and sweatshirts, not hats [46] [CONFIRM: whether any embroidered hat qualifies]. Without it, one side of the border always gets an import.
- **One company, steadier quality.** On Printify, quality depends on which shop you pick [67].
- **Prices are close.** For the same hats, list prices are within about US$2.50 of each other, in both directions [1][2][3][35][36][59]. Printify's paid Premium plan cuts its prices further [58]. Printful charges US$6.50 once per design to digitize, so about US$55 for 8 front designs and one side symbol, more if a design moves to another hat type. Printify digitizes free [8][42].
- **More of what a quiet brand needs.** Side and back embroidery at a set price, for the small clock symbol [68][74]. A free logo on the packing slip [30]. An option to dye thread closer to the brand's own colours, which matters because neither partner's standard threads match them (see the colour table) [9][10].
- **Margins look workable.** Printful lists custom hats in Canada from C$19.06 before shipping and digitizing [18], in line with the C$18–24 base cost in PRODUCTS.md [CONFIRM: CAD price of each chosen hat with embroidery].

What could change this:

- [CONFIRM: in Printful's catalog, set the region to Canada and open the Availability tab on each chosen hat [70].] If they aren't made in Ontario, Canadian orders come from the US. Printful's Canada-only DDP option would still keep duty bills off the customer's doorstep [23]; no such option was found at Printify.
- If Canadian buyers matter most, Printify with Duplium, a Canadian shop, embroiders the Richardson 112 trucker from US$15.71 [36][45]. But US buyers would then get an import, and the US has given no duty-free entry to low-value parcels from any country since 29 August 2025 [63][64].

Before launch, with either partner (spending needs the owner's approval):

- Order samples of the top 3 hats and approve the stitching on the real blank, as `designs/mark/final/the-o/notes.md` requires.
- Settle the thread question in the colour table: use the nearest standard threads, or test Printful's unlimited colour embroidery (+US$3.95 a hat) [9].

Border rules (both partners): a courier parcel from the US enters Canada duty-free up to C$150 and tax-free up to C$40; by post the limit stays C$20 [61][62]. One C$34.99 hat sits just under the C$40 tax line; two hats are over it.

What this means for the store:

- **Return policy.** Both partners cover misprints, damage and lost parcels, and neither takes back a change of mind [27][51]. So: hats are made to order; free replacement or refund for a misprint, damage or a lost parcel reported with a photo within [CONFIRM: owner picks the window; under 30 days leaves time to file the claim] days of delivery; no change-of-mind returns, or the store pays for them.
- **Shipping at checkout.** Live rates need Shopify Advanced, or Grow with the add-on (Shopify includes it with annual billing) [60]. On a lower plan, set flat CAD rates by hand, which also fits the "free shipping over $75" idea in PRODUCTS.md.
- **Design files.** The hat-front lockup at cap 7 mm meets Printful's 6.35 mm text minimum [12] but sits under Printify's 7.6 mm uppercase guide [39]. BRAND.md allows text down to 4.5 mm; both partners ask for 6.35 mm, and Printify resizes or removes anything under 4.57 mm [12][39][40]. Gaps in the logo run 1.0–1.5 mm (notes.md), well under the 3 mm at which Printful says separate shapes may be joined by connecting stitches [13]; check the letter gaps and the channel on the sample. The lockup (62.6 × 16.5 mm) and side master (8.4 × 16.6 mm) fit every area listed above.
