# Comfort Colors blanks on Printful, embroidered

Research for OFF SHFT, 29 October 2026. Six Comfort Colors (CC) blanks the owner picked. Canada and US only. Nothing here spends money or changes the store.

**How this was checked.** printful.com, comfortcolors.com and the blank wholesalers (S&S, Jiffy, BlankStyle and others) could not be opened from this session; the network blocks them. So the Printful facts come from search-result snippets that quote Printful's own pages, and from copies of Printful's catalogue data in public code on GitHub. Snippets are snapshots with no date shown, so prices may have moved. The session's web-search allowance ran out before every gap was closed. The gaps are marked.

- **confirmed** means seen for this exact product: on Printful's page for Printful facts, or on Comfort Colors' spec sheet for fabric facts.
- **to confirm** means not found, found only for another product or seller, or found only in a third-party copy. Check it on Printful's product page or in its editor, with Embroidery selected and the region set to Canada or the US.
- Prices are Printful's listed US$ prices, "one print included". The C$ figures are estimates at C$1.42 per US$1, the rate used in store/research/apparel.md. Printful's own C$ prices were not found; apparel.md says they run about 4% higher.
- Where Printful makes these: not seen for any of the six (being researched separately). The 6030 page says its blank comes from Honduras.

## How the colour matches work

- Printful shows each colour as a swatch with a hex value. For the 1717 (all 45 colours) and part of the 6030, the values come from copies of Printful's catalogue on GitHub (to confirm). For the other blanks, the 1717 swatch with the same colour name stands in (to confirm).
- ΔE is the CIEDE2000 difference between that hex and the OFF SHFT hex, computed for this note. Under 6 is close. 6 to 10 is fair. Over 10 is a different colour.
- Hex swatches are screen colours, not dyed cloth. Garment-dyed shades also change from lot to lot. Other published charts disagree with Printful on the dark colours (see section 1). Treat every match as "to confirm" until a swatch or sample is in hand.

The 1567 is the heavier, warmer cotton-poly hoodie: its fabric is about 50% heavier (9.5 against 6.4 oz/yd²). It is confirmed for embroidery. The 1467 is a lighter all-cotton hoodie and lists about US$10 less. Its embroidery is not confirmed. Both are relaxed fits.

### Are the 1467 and 1469 a matching set?

- **Fabric: yes, on paper.** Both are Comfort Colors' 6.4 oz/yd² (217 g/m²) 100% ring-spun US cotton, garment-dyed (confirmed from both spec sheets).
- **Colours: partly.** Six colours are in both Comfort Colors lists: Blue Jean, Hydrangea, Ivory, Neon Violet, Peachy and Pepper. Espresso is sweatpants only. Black, Butter, Grey and White (and Crimson at some sellers) are hoodie only. Printful's own lists for both: to confirm.
- **Embroidery:** not confirmed on either.
- **Shade:** tops and bottoms are dyed as separate garments, and Printful warns that garment-dyed shades vary (its note on the 1717, section 1, source 4). Check a hoodie and a pair of sweatpants side by side before selling them as a set (to confirm on samples).
- **For OFF SHFT:** only Ivory is near a brand colour (Bone, ΔE 5.9).

### The lightweight fleece family: 1466, 1467 and 1469

- **Same fabric: yes.** All three are Comfort Colors' 6.4 oz/yd² (217 g/m²) 100% ring-spun US cotton, garment-dyed (confirmed from each spec sheet). Printful names all three "fleece".
- **Colours all three share** (Comfort Colors lists): Blue Jean, Hydrangea, Ivory, Neon Violet, Peachy, Pepper. Espresso is on the crewneck and sweatpants, not the hoodie. Printful's lists for all three: to confirm.
- **For a matching crew, hoodie and sweatpants set:** Ivory is the only shared colour near an OFF SHFT colour (Bone). Pepper works as a dark neutral, but it is not a Slate match (ΔE 14.4).
- **The catch:** Printful's embroidery is confirmed only on the 1466 crewneck. An Ivory set works only if Printful also embroiders the 1467 and 1469 and stocks Ivory in all three (to confirm in Printful's editor). The 1469 already shows Ivory in the owner's link.
