## Where Canadian orders ship from

Printful sends each order to its closest shop that has the hat: its US shops, or Mississauga/Toronto in Ontario [16][17]. Which hats the Ontario shop makes isn't confirmed. The check is in Printful's catalog [70]: [CONFIRM: set the region to Canada and open each chosen hat's Availability tab] Then fill the page from the matching row.

|---|---|---|
| Made at the Ontario shop | 3–4 business days [22] | Orders to Canada ship from inside Canada, so there are no duties or import fees. |
| Made in the US, with Printful's Standard DDP on | 3–5 business days, up to 12 to Atlantic Canada [22] | Orders to Canada may ship from the US. Any duties and taxes are paid at checkout, so there's nothing more to pay at the door. |
| Made in the US, no DDP | [CONFIRM: not in the research] | Orders to Canada may ship from the US. If yours does, you may have to pay duties or taxes when it arrives. |

- If only some hats are made in Ontario, a Canadian order can come from either side, so use a US row.
- DDP is Canada-only, and it needs live rates at checkout (see Shipping costs) [23].
- Never call the hats "made in Canada" (AGENTS.md). The page only says where orders ship from.
- If the Canada time changes, change "All in, that's about 1 to 2 weeks" to match. With DDP to Atlantic Canada it can run to 17 business days in all, about three and a half weeks.
