# Home

- **SEO title (40 characters):** OFF SHFT | Hats for the Hours You Earned
- **Meta description (128 characters):** Embroidered hats for people who put in the hours. For fishing, golf, cornhole and the cottage. Made to order and shipped to you.

The home page has no title field of its own. Both lines above go in Online Store → Preferences (title and meta description).

Theme: Horizon, the live theme. Section names are the ones Horizon shows under "Add section" (checked against Horizon's public source, version 4.3.0; an older copy may name a few differently). Every Horizon section arrives with sample text. Replace all of it. Some of it makes claims we can't back up, like "special offers", "work better and last longer" and "manufactured both locally and globally".

## Before you start

- Rename the store to OFF SHFT first (owner). Horizon adds " – " and the store name to the end of every page title unless the title already contains it. Every SEO title in these files contains OFF SHFT, so after the rename nothing is added. Before it, every title ends in "– My Store 2". The footer copyright line shows the store name too.
- There are no product photos yet. They come from the partner's mockups once the hats are set up. Until then the page runs on colour, type and the logo.

## Colours

All from BRAND.md.

- Page background Bone #ECE5D3. Text Ink #1F2733. Buttons Ink with Bone text.
- Dark bands (announcement bar, hero, footer): Slate #27303D background, Bone #ECE5D3 text.
- Rust #B4552D and Sun #D29B3C: small accents only. Rust on Bone is 3.9:1, below the 4.5:1 small text needs, so no Rust body text or links.
- Headings in Unbounded, the logo's font, all caps with wide spacing. [CONFIRM: Unbounded is in Horizon's font picker; if not, use a plain sans] Body text in a plain sans.
