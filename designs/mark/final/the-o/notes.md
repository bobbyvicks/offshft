# OFF SHFT logo: The O

The O in OFF is a clock face. Two hands stand out of it showing 5:00, quitting time, so the name and the idea are one mark: the shift is over and the time is yours. The hands are stitched in the accent thread so they read as clock hands laid on the dial, not as part of the letter.

Owner's picks: hands-only clock (round 1), W3 watch hands, "The O" placement, O 15% taller than the letters.

## Size floors

- Lockup, two colours: cap 6 mm or more (about 62 mm wide). Use cap 7 (about 72 mm wide) on hat fronts.
- Pivot dot: leave it out below cap 6 (two colours) or cap 6.5 (one colour).
- Symbol: 18 mm tall or more. Proof the front symbol at 40 mm, not 50.
- Side of hat: use `side-*.svg` (about 16 mm tall, 7.0 mm ring, 1.5 mm pivot dot, pointed hands 1.5 and 1.6 mm wide). Do not scale the main files down.
- Below cap 6 (sign-offs, straps): set OFF SHFT in plain Archivo Expanded Bold with the font's own O, letter-spacing 0.14 em.

## Notes for the digitizer

- Digitize each size on its own. Never scale a stitch file.
- Stitch order: Ink letters from the centre out (S, H, F, T, then F, F), the ring last, then the accent from the bottom up: pivot dot, hour hand, minute hand. One colour change.
- Ring: satin with edge-run underlay, short stitches on the inner edge. Pull compensation 0.30-0.35 mm, about 0.1 mm more where stitches run horizontally. Digitize the ring about 0.3 mm wider than tall (about 8.6 x 8.3 mm at cap 7) so it sews round on a curved front.
- Hand roots: pull back 0.2 mm, no end compensation, so the channel stays open. Points: a 0.3 mm flat at the tip, pulled back 0.2 mm.
- The S: open both apertures to at least 1.0 mm at the stitched size by shortening the terminals (at cap 7, 0.20 mm off the top terminal, 0.14 mm off the bottom). Check it still reads as an S.
- 6-panel structured caps: the centre seam crosses the S. Either shift the lockup 5.4 mm right so the seam falls in the word space, or open the S as above and give it centre-run plus edge-run underlay.
- Approve only after a sew-out on the actual blank shows the pivot centred within 0.2 mm and both channels at least 0.8 mm open.

## How it was tested

- Two blind reading rounds (20 reads): the two-colour version with long, slim hands read first as a clock face at about 5 o'clock; one-colour and short hands drifted towards "Q" or "magnifying glass".
- A brand review and an embroidery review set every number above.
