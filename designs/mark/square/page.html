The clock in a square

The clock is twice as tall as it is wide, because the hands stand outside the ring. Fit that into a square and the ring ends up about a third of the space. Here are five ways around it, at the sizes they'll really show up: profile pictures, an app icon and the browser tab.

Each one went to three people who'd never seen the logo. They saw one picture each and said what it was.
