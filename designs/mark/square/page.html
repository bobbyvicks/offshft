The clock in a square

The clock is twice as tall as it is wide because the hands stand outside the ring. With the hands outside, the ring can never be more than about a third of a square, however it's placed. Making it bigger means cropping or shortening the hands, and both made it read worse in testing.

So there are really two ways to go. Eighteen people who'd never seen the logo each looked at one picture and said what it was, and three design reviewers went through every option.

Two ways to go

My pick

Keep the hands outside, and put the name in squares

Profile pictures, the app icon and Shopify get OFF over SHFT, with the clock as the O. The browser tab gets the hat clock redrawn pixel by pixel. Same clock as the hats everywhere.

The clock alone, when you want it
  • 3 of 3 read it as OFF SHFT, and all 3 saw the clock in the O without being asked.
  • It's the hat logo restacked: nothing new to learn or keep in step.
  • The clock is letter-sized here. Under about 40 px it's a word shape, but the app always prints the name beside it.
  • The tab icon at 16 px is still a ring with two bars. It's crisp, but it's small.

Put the hands inside, on screens only

On screens the ring becomes the dial and the hands sit inside it, so the clock fills the square. Hats keep the hands outside.
