# Thin O and inside ticks: tested, not adopted

**Correction:** these readers were not context-free. The tool that ran them also showed each reader the owner's
latest chat message ("what if we did inside ticks like how a clock actually works" for the tick rounds, "make the
o less think to accomidate?" for the thin-O round). Treat the numbers below as informed opinions, not blind reads.
Truly blind reads of the final logo are in `../clean-reads/RESULTS.md`.

The owner asked whether tick marks inside the O (like a real clock face) would help, and whether a thinner O
would make room for them. Both were drawn (`build.cjs`, options `wallFactor` and `ticks` in
`../final/the-o/build.cjs`) and read by fresh readers, three per version.

| Version | Read as | Avg. confidence | What readers saw |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current lockup (thick O, no ticks) | OFF SHFT 3/3 | 9 | clock face |
| Thin O + 3 ticks, lockup | OFF SHFT 3/3 | 8 | "crosshair" in 3 of 3 |
| Thin O + 4 ticks, lockup | OFF SHFT 3/3 | 8 | "crosshair" / "scope" in 3 of 3 |
| Current symbol | clock 3/3 | 6 | |
| Thin O symbol, no ticks | clock 3/3 | 6.3 | no meaningful difference |
| Thin O + 4 ticks, symbol | crosshair first 2/3 | 4.3 | "crosshair", "target scope" |
| Thick O + 4 or 12 ticks, 40 mm symbol | clock 3/3 | 6.7 | "gear", "crosshair" mentions |
| Thick O + 4 ticks, print lockup | OFF SHFT 3/3 | 8.3 | "crosshair", "QFF" mentions |

Decision: no ticks; keep the thick O (side walls = letter stem). A ring with four inward ticks and a centre
dot reads as a rifle-scope reticle, which is the wrong association for the brand.

Also tested and not adopted: hands running from the centre over the O (`../final/the-o/hands-centre/`):
lockup 8/10 with "QFF" mentions against 9/10 with none.

The symbol's remaining "magnifying glass" reading comes from the hour hand outside the O at lower right,
with or without ticks. It reads as a clock first in every test; it is strongest next to the full logo.
