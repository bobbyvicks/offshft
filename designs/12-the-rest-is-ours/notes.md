# THE REST IS OURS. (The Rest Is Ours. Hoodie)

On one line. The only accent is the full stop, in the logo’s accent thread: Rust on light fabric, Sun on dark, as on Punched Out. For the The Rest Is Ours. Hoodie (C$89.99): Hoodie, upper back (printed): Lightweight (Comfort Colors 1467), S to 3XL.

Changes from the font (each passed to the logo’s `glyphMM` as a per-letter table, so the logo itself is unchanged):

- **E, straight stem** (Punched Out.’s): the F’s and H’s stem with the E’s own arms and bar, so its stem matches the other letters’. Unbounded bends it; Punched Out.’s cold readers all flagged the bent one. No slot trim at this size: the slots are 2.27 mm as the font draws them.
- **S, this set’s S.** Its terminals are cut flat 0.91 mm (upper) and 1.03 mm (lower) higher than the font’s, the same cut at every size (faces at 572 and 186 font units; the logo’s at cap 7 is 0.36 / 0.41 mm). At this size its inside edges also move back 0.04 mm, less near the outline so the silhouette and the shoulders beside the apertures stay where they were: the counters’ arcs and rounded ends take the full move, and the spine’s two edges take it by a straight ramp, each from none at one end of the spine to the full move at the end that faces an aperture, so the spine stays straight and even. Apertures 2.12 / 2.14 mm and counters at least 2.18 mm where they narrow (the logo’s S: 1.24 / 1.17 and 2.14 mm). Arcs 2.72 / 2.72 mm and spine 2.87 mm, against the font’s 2.75 / 2.75 and 2.91: the spine stays thicker than the arcs, as in the font. Its thinnest stroke, 2.36 mm, is at the upper terminal’s end, where the flat cut meets the outer curve. (Moving every inside edge by the full move thins the spine from both sides, under the arcs; moving only the arcs leaves the apertures under 1.27 mm; cutting the terminals higher instead thins their tips under 1.27 mm.) The logo’s S is unchanged.
- Kerning on top of the font’s spacing (in cap heights): T-H -0.020 (this set’s), E-S -0.055 (this set’s), S-T -0.035 (the font’s own: -26 units), T-space -0.041 (the font’s own: -31 units), S-space -0.040 (this set’s) and R-S -0.045 (the font’s own -4 units and this set’s -0.040). kit.text drops the font’s own kerning (opentype.js 1.3.4 can’t read its extension lookups), so the build reads it from the font’s GPOS table; Punched Out.’s pairs are its own; this set’s own pairs are judged on the true-size proofs and on the white between each pair, and `build.cjs` gives the reason for each (`EXTRA_KERN`). Nothing else is kerned.

## Notes for the digitizer

- The listing has this one printed (Hoodie, upper back (printed): Lightweight (Comfort Colors 1467)): the SVG is the print file as it stands, letters and full stop in the same two colours. The notes below are for an embroidered version, which the floors and checks allow.
- Digitize each size on its own and never scale a stitch file. This is its own drawing at cap 12.9, not a cap-7 file scaled up (the cap-7 trims shrink with size and are none here; the S’s shorter terminals are part of the letters’ drawing and stay).
- Satin throughout, 3.56-3.58 mm stems. One colour change: the letters, then the full stop.
- Stitch order, centre out, then the colour change and the full stop: T, I, S, O, U, R, S, then S, E, R, E, H, T.
- Pull compensation: 0.15-0.18 mm per edge (0.30-0.35 mm total), plus 0.05 mm per edge where stitches run horizontally (the stems), as for the logo. Standard compensation everywhere: the narrowest opening is 2.12 mm (the S).
- S: the artwork already includes the shorter terminals and the moved-back inside edges. Pull both terminal ends back 0.2 mm with no end compensation, as for the logo’s S.
- R: the notch between its stem and leg is 1.88 mm wide where it meets the bowl and opens out below; 0.05 mm compensation on its faces, so only its top fills.
- Full stop: 3.96 mm across: satin, or a fill if it looks loose; straight after the colour change. It has 3.55 mm of fabric to the S.
- Fleece: water-soluble topping, so the loft doesn’t swallow the openings and the crotches.
- Upper back: stems 3.56-3.58 mm, under the 4.4 mm columns the logo sews in plain satin (its 45 mm symbol), so plain satin should hold; split the stems if the sew-out shows long, loose stitches. A rough stitch count from the ink area (1317.3 mm², 2.04 sq in at 1,200-2,400 stitches per square inch of satin): about 2,500-5,000, under the 15,000 at which Printful’s large-embroidery stitch charge starts (store/research/caps-and-hoodie.md).
- Approve only after a sew-out on the actual blank shows both E slots at least 1.0 mm open and both S apertures at least 1.0 mm open and its counters open.
