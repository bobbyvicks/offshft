## Clearance check

Medium. No "Mulligan Monday" brand found, but "MULLIGAN" alone is a registered US trademark for hats (Reg. 5320102, Mulligan Gear LLC). Using the word as part of a slogan is usually lower risk than using it as a brand, but check before selling, ideally with a trademark lawyer.

Keep it and get it checked, or swap for a backlog golf hat (e.g. Back Nine Shift).
