# Phrase ideas, round 2 (30 Oct 2026)

Written when the owner asked "what else could we add?" after the six round-1 phrases went up. Aimed at the gaps the 12
lines in the store leave: night shift, rotations away from home, retirement and gifts, and winter toques. Two writers
(Q4 and gifts; shift patterns), a strict editor, then a trademark knock-out screen and a double-check
(store/research/trademarks-round-2.md: search results only, not legal advice; check USPTO and CIPO before ordering
samples). Widths are measured with Punched Out. v3's setLine at 7 mm caps; the phrase build's own kerning sets each
line 0.2 to 1.2 mm narrower. No previews or art yet: nothing is drafted until the owner picks.

## The editor's three

**LAST SHIFT.** I've worked my last shift: I'm retired, and my days are my own now.
- For: retirement gifts from coworkers, kids and partners. It says retirement in the job's own words, not RETIRED.
- Where: dad hat front, one line, 80.4 x 7.3 mm (79.2 with the build's kerning), moved 4.0 mm to the wearer's left so the
  centre seam runs down the word space. Or the cuffed beanie, stacked LAST / SHIFT. (41.2 x 18.0 mm). The stack has no
  gap a 6-panel seam can run down, so not stacked on a 6-panel.
- Watch: never THE LAST SHIFT. (two films; 111.5 mm). "Last shift" is also the news phrase for plant closures and, in
  retail, the closing shift, so the title and copy say retirement.
- Trademark: small risk. Nothing filed for LAST SHIFT; SHIFT on its own is crowded on hats (see the screen).

**DONE NIGHTS.** My run of night shifts is over, so the next few days are mine.
- For: night-shift nurses, plant and mill crews, security, drivers: the biggest group with no line yet (5:00 and SUN’S
  STILL UP. are day-shift lines). It's also the first winter toque line.
- Where: cuffed beanie (1501KC, not the ribbed RIO), stacked DONE / NIGHTS. (54.1 x 18.0 mm). Or a 5-panel front on one
  line (96.2 mm; 96.0 with the build's kerning). On a 6-panel it moves 3.0 mm to the wearer's right, so the seam runs
  between the N and the I (a 2.43 mm gap, like Punched Out.'s H-E); not Banked Time.'s shift.
- Watch: Canadian grammar like "done work"; a few Americans may read "fed up with nights". Keep it off pieces that
  carry PUNCHED OUT. or PARKED.
- Trademark: looks clear.

**HOME FOR A BIT.** I've been away working, on the road or at camp, and I'm home for a while before I go back.
- For: camp and fly-in rotations, long-haul drivers, travelling trades and linemen, travel nurses, and their families.
- Where: 5-panel front, stacked HOME / FOR A BIT. (67.5 x 18.0 mm; 66.8 with the build's kerning). Or the M2475
  quarter-zip's left chest, stacked. Not on the 6606 trucker or any 6-panel: no gap runs clear through both lines near
  the centre.
- Watch: the copy has to say it's the break between hitches or runs. It's one word off Spirit of the West's "Home for a
  Rest": don't nod to it. A second drivers' line after PARKED., so keep the two on different hats.
- Trademark: looks clear. One untitled Amazon Music track (B0FPQFGGTX) matched the phrase; open it before launch.
