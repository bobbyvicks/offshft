## Notes for the digitizer

- Digitize each size on its own and never scale a stitch file.
- Satin throughout, 1.88-1.94 mm stems. One colour change: the letters (the apostrophe with them), then the full stop.
- Stitch order, centre out (bottom line first, as caps sew centre out and bottom up), then the colour change and the full stop: STILL UP.: L, L, U, P, then I, T, S; SUN’S: N, ’, S, then U, S.
- Pull compensation: 0.15-0.18 mm per edge (0.30-0.35 mm total), plus 0.05 mm per edge where stitches run horizontally (the stems), as for the logo. Only 0.05 mm per edge on the faces of the S’s apertures and the S’s counters, so they finish at about 1.19 and 1.22 mm (with full compensation they’d close to about 0.93 and 0.96 mm).
- S: the artwork already includes the shorter terminals and the moved-back inside edges. Pull both terminal ends back 0.2 mm with no end compensation, as for the logo’s S.
- ’: one satin shape in the letter thread, sewn with the letters; 0.10 mm compensation, as for the full stop. It sits 1.52 mm from the N and 1.50 mm from the S.
- Crotches: the N’s two 35.1-degree crotches, where the diagonal meets each stem (thread fills about 1.6-1.8 mm of each). Stop the diagonals 0.3 mm short of the crotch points, with no compensation on the crotch faces.
- T: its bar’s end faces the S (1.65 mm), as the logo’s F-T faces 1.47 mm. Pull that end back 0.15 mm with no end compensation.
- Full stop: satin dot with 0.10 mm compensation, straight after the colour change. It has 2.02 mm of fabric to the P.
- Seamless fronts: centred.
- Approve only after a sew-out on the actual blank shows both S apertures at least 1.0 mm open and its counters open, the crotches of the N open and the apostrophe’s tail clear, so it reads as an apostrophe, not a dot.
