# OFF SHFT — brand guidelines

Version 1.0 · 28 October 2026
**Status: final selected source artwork; proposed application system.**

These guidelines preserve the owner's chosen “The O” logo. They extend its use to hats, oversized hoodies and baggy sweats, with labels and packaging to match. Garment shapes and quality are product-development goals until a supplier and physical samples confirm them. No blank, fabric specification or manufacturing result is established by a visual mockup.

## The brand

OFF SHFT is for the time you have earned. Work ends; your time begins. Days off can fall on any day of the week.

Keep the identity quiet: a small, legible mark, generous empty space and a few useful details. The clothes should carry the brand comfortably without becoming a billboard. The current range is hats, oversized hoodies and baggy sweats; the older hat-only framing in the source brief is superseded for this application system by the owner's current apparel direction.

Write **OFF SHFT**: two words, all caps, no “I.” Use that spelling in headlines, labels, packaging and running text. Do not join the words or expand SHFT to SHIFT.

Use short, plain sentences. Respect people's work and celebrate time off. Avoid hustle language, invented product benefits and exaggerated claims. The mark needs no slogan. **“Your time.” is optional proposed copy, not an approved slogan or part of the logo.** Keep it separate from the mark and off production files until the owner chooses to use it.

## The selected identity

The full logo is the primary identifier. Its O is a clock, with hands at 5:00, built into the Unbounded Bold lettering. The clock ring remains the same height as the letter O; the stand-alone symbol has its own slightly larger ring proportion. Preserve the source paths, spacing, hand positions and pivot treatment.

| Role | Source artwork | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary logo | `designs/mark/final/the-o/logo-light.svg` and `logo-dark.svg` | Default garment branding and first brand introduction |
| Secondary symbol | `designs/mark/final/the-o/symbol-light.svg` and `symbol-dark.svg` | Small supporting placement, patch or avatar; the supplied masters are 25 mm tall |
| Small master | `designs/mark/final/the-o/side-light.svg` and `side-dark.svg` | Dedicated small drawing for side placements and small trim trials; use at its supplied size |
| Single-colour alternatives | `logo-one-colour-*.svg`, `symbol-one-colour-*.svg`, `side-one-colour-*.svg` in the same directory | Tonal or genuinely single-colour production and the fabric exceptions below |

The full logo should appear at the main introduction to the brand. The symbol supports recognition; it should not replace every instance of the name. A full front logo and small secondary symbol can share a product, but each needs space of its own.

Never recreate the logo by typing OFF SHFT. Do not redraw the clock, add ticks, change the time, rotate or stretch the artwork, crop a hand, add outlines or effects, or insert a slogan into the lockup. One-colour artwork is a separate master: converting the two-colour SVG to one fill does not create the correct one-colour version. Do not use the rejected `hands-centre/` artwork or the font-comparison files as production sources.

## Colour

These values come from `BRAND.md`. They are digital colour targets, not approved thread codes, ink recipes or fabric standards.

| Colour | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bone | `#ECE5D3` | 236, 229, 211 | Light field and light lettering |
| Ink | `#1F2733` | 31, 39, 51 | Dark lettering and primary text |
| Rust | `#B4552D` | 180, 85, 45 | Clock hands and pivot on light backgrounds |
| Sun | `#D29B3C` | 210, 155, 60 | Clock hands and pivot on dark backgrounds |
| Sage | `#9AA383` | 154, 163, 131 | Supporting colour in layouts; not a replacement logo accent |

Keep Bone and Ink dominant. Use Rust or Sun where the approved logo specifies them. Sage may support packaging or editorial layouts sparingly. Oat, Moss, Spruce and Slate are fabric colour names; do not invent brand HEX values or assume they match Sage or Ink.

| Fabric | Existing logo rule |
|---|---|
| Bone | Ink letters and ring; Rust hands and pivot |
| Slate or Spruce | Bone letters and ring; Sun hands and pivot |
| Oat | Ink + Rust only after a sew-out passes; otherwise the Ink one-colour master |
| Moss or Rust | Bone one-colour master |

Two-colour is the default front logo. The listed fabric exceptions and tonal or single-thread jobs use the dedicated one-colour files. Sample any new fabric colour before adding it to the range.

For packaging, use the same approved light or dark artwork against a suitable contrasting field. A single-ink print job uses the dedicated one-colour master. Match physical output from supplier samples; the display colour is not the approval standard.

## Typography

**Existing choice: Unbounded Bold.** Use it for the mark and short uppercase headlines. The source specifies 0.12 em tracking for brand text. The logo's custom kerning and trimmed S are already built into its vector outlines; ordinary typesetting cannot reproduce it exactly.

**Proposed support face: Archivo.** Use Archivo SemiBold for short explanatory copy, product details and label information, and Archivo Bold for emphasis. Keep support text compact, legible and plainly set. These files are present in `tools/fonts/`; retain their accompanying OFL licences when distributing the fonts.

Unbounded is not the default for paragraphs or small functional copy. The source embroidery floor for Unbounded Bold is 6.6 mm cap height with the specified S trims; another font does not inherit that approval. Small Archivo printing, weaving or embroidery needs a supplier proof at its actual size.

## Clearspace — new recommendation

Let **D** be the outside diameter of the clock's O ring in the actual artwork being placed, excluding its hands. Leave at least **one D** on every side of the artwork's full outer bounds, including the farthest hand tips. Measure from the visible artwork, not from a letter baseline or a page frame.

At the cap-7 full logo, D is approximately 7.30 mm. This creates a reserved area of approximately 77.24 × 32.64 mm around the 62.64 × 18.04 mm artwork. The margin scales with a larger visual placement. For the small side master, measure D from that master rather than borrowing the full logo's diameter.

Keep unrelated text, trim, edges and decorative elements outside this space. If a label cannot accommodate the mark and its clearspace, enlarge the label or use the appropriate secondary master. The cap centre seam follows the separate construction rule in the production handoff. This clearspace rule is a new layout recommendation, not a claim of supplier validation.

## Initial garment placements — proposals for sampling

All dimensions below refer to visible artwork. They are starting points for physical samples, not validated manufacturing specifications. The final placement must be confirmed on the chosen blank and across the size range.

| Product | Initial placement | Starting artwork size | Sampling requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oversized hoodie | Wearer's left chest; full two-colour logo | 80 mm wide, proportional height | Check position on body, drape, pocket and seam clearance; digitize the chosen size separately |
| Baggy sweats | Wearer's left upper thigh; secondary clock symbol | 25 mm total height | Use the dedicated 25 mm symbol; confirm pocket clearance, comfort and placement when worn |
| Hat | Front full logo at cap height 7 mm | 62.64 × 18.04 mm in two colours | For 6-panel caps shift artwork 5.2 mm right in the front view so the centre seam falls in the word space; centre on 5-panel or seamless fronts |

Avoid adding a large back graphic or extra phrase merely to fill space. Any secondary mark should have a clear role and be considered during sample fitting. Exact distances from garment seams belong in the supplier's placement proof after the blank is selected.

## Labels and packaging — application proposals

| Item | Recommended hierarchy | What must be confirmed |
|---|---|---|
| Neck or waistband brand label | Full logo first; size as separate functional information | Finished label dimensions, fold and seam allowances, readability, feel against skin and attachment method |
| Small hem or side label | Dedicated small clock master | Actual-size woven or printed proof; usable face after folds and stitching; no arbitrary shrinking |
| Hangtag | Full logo on the main face; product information on the reverse | Stock, hole position, trim and bleed, contrast, and space for confirmed product data |
| Sticker or closure | Full logo where space allows; symbol as a supporting detail | Finished dimensions, material, adhesive and production proof |
| Mailer or carton | One restrained brand impression and practical shipping area | Supplier dieline, printable area and material; separate transport labels from brand artwork |

Give functional text its own area. Keep care and product-data labels separate from the brand treatment when necessary for legibility. Do not present placeholder fibre content, origin, care instructions, certifications, prices or barcodes as verified product facts. Add only confirmed data for the actual item.

Print, woven-label and embroidery processes have different limits. The embroidery size floors are not verified minimum sizes for print or weaving. Approve the method, substrate, colours and detail reproduction from physical supplier proofs.
