The original SVG inner markup is inserted verbatim. Its outlines, colours, aspect ratio, clock hands and pivot are unchanged. All board typography is Archivo converted to outlines using `tools/kit/kit.cjs`.

These are proposed visual placements and silhouettes, not garment patterns, technical packs, or manufacturing approval. Apparel and cap drawings are shown at different display scales. Hoodie and sweat artwork scales are proportion studies using approximately 0.36 display units per nominal millimetre; the cap uses 0.84. Confirm positions and measurements on the selected garment blanks. The sweat legs have open, wide hems and no jogger cuffs.

The cap illustration has no centre-front seam. For a six-panel blank, follow the canonical notes: shift the lockup 5.2 mm right so the seam falls in the word space. Confirm all versions with a sew-out on the actual blank, following `designs/mark/final/the-o/notes.md`.
