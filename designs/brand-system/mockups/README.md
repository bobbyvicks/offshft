# Brand world concept

`brand-world.png` is an AI-generated material and placement concept created with the built-in image-generation tool. It illustrates the intended feel of bone apparel, slate sweats and headwear, with coordinated paper and woven branding.

This is not a photograph of real stock or a source for manufacturing artwork. AI imagery may approximate logo geometry, type and colour. The exact artwork in `../assets/`, flat application SVGs and garment placement drawings take precedence. Hoodie/sweat construction, fabric, actual colour and the packaging material are not approved specifications.

Reference inputs were the exact canonical `logo-light.png`, `logo-dark.png` and `symbol-light.png` exports in `../assets/`. `prompt.txt` contains the generation prompt. A subsequent correction attempt was reviewed and not selected; the original concept is the delivered image because it better retains the approved colour relationships.
