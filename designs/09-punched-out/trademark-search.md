# Trademark knock-out search: PUNCHED OUT. (US and Canada)

This search was done for OFF SHFT on 2026-10-29, using web search results only.

## In short

No US or Canadian filing turned up for PUNCHED OUT, PUNCHEDOUT, PUNCHED-OUT, PUNCHIN' OUT or PUNCHED OUT CLOTHING, in any class. Where the goods showed for a PUNCH OUT or PUNCH-OUT record, they were never clothing; Nintendo's PUNCH-OUT!! video games are one example. The two closest clothing records are both in the US, and their current status is unknown. One is POWER PUNCHOUT (Reg. 5079893), a boxing-glove design registered in 2016 for caps, hats, hoodies and T-shirts. The other is PUNCHED (Serial 98254855), a duck design filed in 2023 for clothing and headwear. From search results alone, the risk of selling PUNCHED OUT. hats and clothing looks low in Canada and low to moderate in the US, depending mainly on whether those two are still live. This is only a first screen, because no record page could be opened, statuses come from snippets that can be years old, and search engines barely index the Canadian database.

**What to check on the official sites:**

- **US search:** go to https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/ and type `PUNCHED OUT`, then `PUNCH OUT`, `PUNCHOUT`, `PUNCHEDOUT` and `PUNCHED`. Look at live marks in classes 025 (clothing and hats), 026 (patches) and 035 (stores).
- **US status:** enter 86818395 and 98254855 at https://tsdr.uspto.gov/ (the USPTO's status page) to see whether those two are still live.
- **Canada:** go to https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/srch and type `PUNCHED OUT`, `PUNCH OUT`, `PUNCHOUT` and `PUNCHED`. Limit the results to Nice classes 25, 26 and 35.

- **POWER PUNCHOUT (Reg. 5079893): open this record first.**

Its listed goods are athletic apparel, namely, athletic uniforms, Caps, Hats, Hooded sweatshirts, Polo shirts, and T-shirts in International Class 25. Its status is Registered, with a registration date of 2016-11-08."
  - Its goods match yours. The mark itself is different: it adds POWER, it shows a boxing glove and lightning bolts, and it reads as a power punch rather than the end of a shift. "Punched out" can also suggest a fight, though, so don't rely on the meaning alone.
  - One summary says it has claimed use since July 2008.

  - Two summaries named an owner, but the same name shows up on an unrelated POWER record and may belong there. Treat the owner as unknown.
- **PUNCHED (Serial 98254855): the snippets disagree on what kind of mark it is.**
  - One summary calls it a plain word mark, but also describes claimed colours and "a red duck with a wig placed in front of many yellow ducks". Both can't be true.
  - The only status seen dates from 2023. It may since have been registered, refused or abandoned.

## What couldn't be checked

- **No record page was opened.**
  - This environment's network blocks USPTO, CIPO, WIPO, Justia, Trademarkia, Google, Etsy and Amazon.
  - The page-fetch tool also failed ("host not found") on trademark.justia.com, trademarks.justia.com, ised-isde.canada.ca and a hat reseller's site.
  - So every fact above comes from search snippets and search-tool summaries. None is confirmed by the source.
- **Snippets can be old or mixed up.**
  - Statuses are snapshots, some about three years old.
  - Summaries of Justia list pages sometimes took an owner name from the next entry. Two names given for the POWER PUNCHOUT records were dropped for that reason.
  - Some summaries contradict each other (see PUNCHED, 98254855).
- **The search budget ran out.**
  - A shared limit of 200 web searches was used up: 104 on US records, 95 on Canada and WIPO, and 1 on market use. The second review ran no new searches; it re-checked the raw results instead.
  - Shops were reached only through a few general web searches, not searched directly. That includes Etsy, Amazon, Redbubble, TeePublic, Zazzle, Spreadshirt, Instagram, TikTok, other Shopify and Printful stores, and Canadian shops.
- **Canada is the weakest part.**
  - Search engines index the Canadian database poorly, so finding nothing there means little.
  - Whether PUNCH & DESIGN (0413946) was renewed is unknown.
- **New filings** from 2025 and 2026 may not be indexed yet.
- **Unregistered (common-law) use** is not in any register, which is why the "In use on clothing" section exists. Only a light check of it was possible.
