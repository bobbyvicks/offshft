# OFF SHFT — next round research

29 October 2026. Research and proposed directions only; no artwork generated, no product changes, and no trademark clearance claimed.

Recommend developing **TIME WELL EARNED**, **THE REST IS OURS**, and **OURS TO SPEND**, in that order, plus a restrained **5:00** study. The first phrase says the brand idea most directly; the second adds a quiet double meaning; the third suggests choosing how to spend the hours after work. These are new proposals, not owner-approved phrases.

Read [AGENTS](../AGENTS.md), [README](../README.md), [BRAND](../BRAND.md), [DESIGNS](../DESIGNS.md) and the [final artwork notes](mark/final/the-o/notes.md). **Search scope and limits**

Each of the four phrase candidates was searched in quotation marks with apparel terms such as `shirt`, `hat`, `clothing` and `apparel`, plus marketplace restrictions covering Etsy and Amazon. The first three candidates originally screened (TIME WELL EARNED, THE REST IS OURS, HOURS ARE OURS) also received Redbubble/TeePublic checks and near-phrase follow-ups. Official indexed checks used the exact quoted phrase with `site:uspto.gov`, `site:ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search`, or domain filters for `uspto.gov`, `ised-isde.canada.ca` and `ic.gc.ca`. OURS TO SPEND received exact-phrase official domain searches. Numeric searches included `5:00`, `FIVE OCLOCK`, `FIVE O'CLOCK` and `5 O'CLOCK` with apparel terms.

This was a search-engine-indexed screening, not a completed search inside either registry. An empty result does not establish availability, distinctiveness or permission. Before commercial use, search exact and similar wording in the live systems and assess related goods and common-law use. [USPTO guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) includes both registry and broader commercial searches; [CIPO guidance](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/trademarks/additional-search-options) also calls for phonetic equivalents, synonyms and English/French translations. Owner selection and a proper clearance review remain separate from this creative shortlist.
